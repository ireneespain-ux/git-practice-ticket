A fun fact about Barcelona: Barcelona's famous sun-soaked beaches are completely artificial
As part of a massive urban renewal project ahead of the 1992 Games, the city cleared out the seaside industries and imported hundreds of tons of sand—much of it shipped directly from Egypt—to build the vibrant 4.5-kilometer beachfront that exists today.
